# Word choice

The Dictionary in Issue 9 has 875 approved words and 1,274 unapproved words with alternatives. ASD holds the copyright, so this file does not copy it. This file gives common substitutions that we confirmed against Issue 9. It also gives the words with a restricted meaning or part of speech, and tells what to do when you cannot check a word.

Contents: When you cannot check · Common substitutions · Part of speech · Meaning traps · -ing words · Not STE rules

## When you cannot check

The shortest everyday word is often approved, but not always. `enough` is not approved and `sufficient` is. `about` means only "concerned with". For a quantity, use `approximately`. So do not assume that a simple word is approved.

1. Prefer the substitutions below.
2. When no approved word fits, rebuild the sentence before you use a technical noun or verb.
3. In the report, list each word that you did not confirm.
4. When the user needs strict STE, ask for the Dictionary. The official copy is free at asd-ste100.org.

## Meaning traps

| Word | Approved meaning | For the other meaning |
|---|---|---|
| follow | come after, go after | obey (rules, instructions) |
| since | from a time | because (cause) |
| while | at the same time | rebuild with but (contrast) |
| as | preposition | because, while (as a conjunction) |
| about | concerned with | approximately (a quantity) |
| turn | rotate | rebuild the sentence |
| see | with the eyes | rebuild the sentence (a cross-reference) |
| above, below | position | more than, less than (a limit) |
| must | an obligation | in a procedure, only for safety or an important condition; otherwise the imperative |

`may` and `should` cause the most damage. A reader cannot tell whether "the cable should be disconnected" is an instruction or an opinion. Decide which it is. Write `must` or the imperative for an instruction. For real uncertainty, write `can` or `possibly`, and keep the uncertainty in the sentence.

## -ing words

The approved `-ing` words are:

- Nouns: `lighting`, `opening`, `routing`, `servicing`
- Adjectives: `mating`, `missing`, `remaining`
- Pronoun: `something`
- Preposition: `during`

Any other `-ing` word can appear only as a technical noun or inside one, such as "welding torch".

## Not STE rules

`and/or`, number style, and unit spacing are house-style matters. A general recommendation (GR-6) discourages `etc.` and other Latin abbreviations, but no rule forbids them. Apply the house style, and do not report these as STE violations.
